Once upon a time, in a small Indian village, six blind men lived together. One day, they heard that a royal elephant had arrived at the village temple.
Curious to discover what an elephant looked like, they went to see it.
The first man touched its huge side and exclaimed, “An elephant is just like a giant wall!”
The second touched its sharp tusk and said, “No! It is like a spear!”
The third held its long, twisting trunk and shouted, “You are all wrong! It is just like a snake!”
The fourth felt its strong leg and said, “Surely, an elephant is like a tree!”
The fifth touched its large ear and declared, “It is clearly like a hand fan!”
Finally, the sixth held its swinging tail and laughed, “How can you not see? It is just like a rope!”
Soon, the six men began arguing loudly, each believing he was right.
A wise old villager passing by smiled and said, “You are all partly right, but none of you understands the whole elephant. You have each felt only one part!”
The six men fell silent, realising their mistake.
Moral: We should never judge the whole picture from just one perspective. Sometimes, everyone has a little piece of the truth!
